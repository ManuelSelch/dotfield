# Defender Borders — Requirements

## Goal

Make an economy-first opening defensible without requiring the player to win both Courtyard bridges immediately. Extend the existing Defender with Gatehouse-style ground transit control: attackers must capture a blocking Defender or find a legal way around its territory.

The user explicitly requested keeping the current Defender's regenerating shields and lack of troop production, while adding enemy-crossing prevention. This replaces the idea of adding a separate Gatehouse role; it is not authorization to add a sixth role or change shield balance. Status: specifications only, not implemented. Boundary and blocked-order visuals require an isolated mockup and explicit approval before production UI changes.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Extend the existing `defender` city role on every layout, with Relay On or Off and independently hostile factions.
- Ground transit blocking through the Defender's full logical territory region, normal direct attacks on the Defender and manually staged alternatives around it.
- Borders formed by multiple player-built Defenders, including use alongside mountains, river crossings and the map boundary.
- Shared legality, launch-queue handling, live/forecast simulation, AI, worker action revalidation, save/replay compatibility and accessible feedback.
- Preserve Defender cost, shields, nonproduction, role conversion and existing capture behavior.

### Out of Scope
- A separate Gatehouse city type, fortification attachment, extra Home switch or new map generator.
- Freely placed walls, manually drawn borders, moving cities or paid upgrades to whole territory regions.
- Extra armour, shield capacity/recharge changes, free shield charges, production on Defenders or additional damage/interception weapons.
- Automatic curved ground routes, automatic retargeting to the blocker or silently capturing an intermediate city.
- Blocking aircraft, changing Artillery line of sight, stopping bullets with region boundaries or changing normal troop collision geometry.
- Guaranteed wins against stronger opponents, unbreakable borders or claims of balanced economy-versus-rush openings without playtesting.

## Requirements

- **R1 — Enhance the existing role.** Keep the serialized city type `defender` and the existing five-role system. A player converts an owned city at runtime through the current control strip; no new build placement or currency. Preserve the current20-troop cost, requirement to leave at least one troop, current-role no-op, no refunds and existing conversion restrictions. The visible name is an open question, not a reason to create another role identity.
- **R2 — Preserve the shield/economy contract.** Defenders produce no troops, including on Factory hosts. Retain one batch-blocking shield per10 active seconds, capacity3, the shared Capture Shield pool and max-not-sum coordination with Defensive Network. Keep stored excess capture charges, existing conversion/recharge reset rules and no free charges on conversion. A blocking Defender does not receive extra health, troop growth or range damage. The existing [city-role behavior](../../docs/city-conversion/IMPLEMENTATION.md) remains authoritative for these unchanged mechanics.
- **R3 — Region-based transit control.** A non-neutral Defender controls its complete territory region, not merely its city circle or an arbitrary radius. Friendly ground orders may pass through it. A hostile ground order whose direct route crosses that region on the way to another city is illegal. This applies equally to attacks and reinforcements, every faction, and destinations on either side of the border. Current owner and role determine access; stored shields, shield recharge eligibility and garrison size do not. A zero-garrison owned Defender still controls its region until captured or converted.
- **R4 — Attack the obstacle, not through it.** A hostile faction may target the blocking Defender directly if its approach is otherwise legal. Ignore only that destination Defender's own transit restriction; do not ignore another blocking Defender encountered earlier on the route. Terrain and river restrictions still apply. If multiple Defenders block a route, feedback identifies the first encountered blocker with stable tie handling. Rejected orders must not deduct/reserve troops, replace a valid existing queue or auto-dispatch to a different city.
- **R5 — Buildable borders, not invulnerability.** Connected Defender regions can close a ground approach; uncovered regions and routes around the ends remain usable. Mountains, water and the field edge retain their existing restrictions and may complete a defensive line. Regions that meet only at a point must not be presented as a guaranteed sealed border. Neither a Defender nor a chain gains an attack-range bonus. Attackers can open a breach by capturing a frontline Defender; its role remains Defender for the captor, and access switches by faction. Ground forces already inside a protected area may continue using routes that cross no hostile Defender region.
- **R6 — Stable logical geometry.** Derive blocking regions from the same city positions, Voronoi clipping and separate river-bank regions as the visible map, in the fixed360×480 logical field. A change in ownership or role changes access, not region shape. Render size, aspect-ratio margins, device pixel ratio, focus/selection and animation must not alter legality. Keep city-circle impacts and existing ground collision geometry unchanged. Region boundaries now have a deliberate additional gameplay meaning only for Defenders; the old rule that all Voronoi boundaries are cosmetic is superseded for this feature. Specify and test the proposed edge/tangency policy below before implementation.
- **R7 — Dynamic changes and committed movement.** Building, converting away from or capturing a Defender must update subsequent order legality immediately at the command/event timestamp. Already-launched ground troops finish their committed route under ordinary travel/combat rules: no new instant kills, teleporting, bounce-back or forced retargeting. They may still lose troops to existing collisions or weapons. The proposed cancellation of not-yet-launched batches is an open question below; once chosen, apply it consistently at conversion/capture events and before launches, including stale worker actions. Do not duplicate troops, refund launched batches, grant faction survival to cancelled reservations or preserve a stale permission after ownership changes.
- **R8 — Explicit staging and aircraft counterplay.** A rejected direct order requires the player to choose another legal city-to-city leg or attack the Defender; do not automatically route around it. Aircraft and embarked cargo retain current region/terrain immunity, including flights directly to cities behind a border and normal air attacks on the Defender itself. The final destination's real owner, garrison and shields still resolve ground arrivals/air pulses normally. Artillery and Watchtower targeting retain their existing rules; this feature restricts ground transit only.
- **R9 — One legality contract everywhere.** Ground commands, drag/click/keyboard feedback, launch-time queue checks, AI candidate generation, strategic distances, known-order forecasts and worker action application must agree on current Defender access. A route blocked for the player must also be blocked for AI, with no hidden bypass. Forecasts must handle a known capture opening/closing a border at the actual event time and the selected committed/queued policy, not judge an entire interval using only its final ownership. Never assume future opponent conversions/orders.
- **R10 — Credible AI counterplay and construction.** AI must attack feasible blocking Defenders, stage around open ends, use available aircraft where useful and reinforce useful border cities without abandoning its home or relay priorities blindly. It may construct multiple Defenders to form a border; the current blanket one-Defender construction heuristic must not prevent this. Preserve producer-base safeguards and the limits on other self-built roles; production sacrificed and conversion cost remain real trade-offs. Do not build redundant borders, repeatedly switch roles or attempt clearly impossible breaches. Blocked ground distance is not proof a destination is unreachable by air. Verify independent faction identities and synchronous/worker parity.
- **R11 — Relay and upgrade coexistence.** Converting a relay city to Defender does not remove the objective, reset scores or alter ownership-based scoring. A shield-depleted Defender still blocks transit. Existing Defensive Network membership/recharge uses its established adjacency rules; transit access must not silently redefine network connectivity. Preserve Factory, Industry, Slow Zone, Capture Shield, aircraft accounting and home-defense safeguards.
- **R12 — Save and lifecycle continuity.** Preserve exact roles, owners, opening, queues, airborne troops, shield timers and relay progress through pause/hidden/reload/restart, without offline catch-up. Keep event-only saves and safe corrupt-save fallback. Do not reject a legitimate committed flight solely because a Defender built later now blocks its route. Do not infer the historical legality of queued/airborne orders from current ownership alone. No sixth-role migration or geometry serialization is required by the product rules; whether old saved rounds gain the new blocking policy is explicitly unresolved below. Restart/replay must reproduce the opening under the round's selected rules policy, not silently change policy mid-round.
- **R13 — Readable, accessible boundary feedback.** Mock up a recognizable fortified-region boundary, valid attacks on the Defender and blocked routes to cities behind it. Visually distinguish this from the existing Defensive Network perimeter and relay marker without obscuring city counts, role/shield badges or ordinary ownership. Explain the blocker by city identity and offer the concept “attack it or stage around it”; do not promise an automatic detour. Drag, mouse and keyboard feedback must agree; include accessible role/region descriptions, visible focus, minimum44px new interactive targets and bounded announcements. Preserve the five-icon66px city strip,12px gap and stable Canvas geometry across selection/conversion/blocked/paused/terminal states. No new gameplay controls or decorative animation are required.
- **R14 — Acceptance evidence.** Start each production slice with deterministic failing tests. Cover isolated and chained Defenders, direct target exceptions, multiple blockers, friendly passage, every hostile faction, gaps, both sides of a border, boundary cases, rivers/mountains, zero garrisons/shields, role/capture changes and ground/air counterplay. Verify before/at/after-event queue policies, live/forecast/worker agreement, troop conservation, saves and legacy behavior. Browser checks must cover all input modes, multi-Defender borders, relay coexistence and stable geometry at320px,430px and desktop widths. Playtest the Courtyard southern passage and an open-field economy-first border; do not infer balance from blocked-order tests.

## Assumptions

These are recommendations, not additional confirmed user decisions:
- Retain the visible name **Defender**, its icon and serialized identity; describe its added function as blocking enemy ground transit. “Gate House” describes the behavior unless the user requests a UI rename.
- Neutral Defenders remain inactive and do not block any faction, consistent with their inactive recharge. Non-neutral owned Defenders block every other faction, not only the human or a selected enemy. Confirm neutral behavior below because the older, separate Gatehouse proposal instead locked neutral crossings.
- When a region becomes hostile/blocking, cancel now-illegal **unlaunched** batches and release their reservations; their troops remain at the source. Keep launched batches unchanged. Do not auto-resume a cancelled queue when the border later opens; a new order is needed. This follows the earlier Gatehouse queue recommendation but still needs explicit confirmation for the Defender extension.
- Initial geometry recommendation: classify the actual direct ground route in logical coordinates, using a documented simulation-owned segment test, not screen pixels. Entering a hostile region's interior blocks transit; isolated vertex tangency does not. Running along its boundary for positive distance is blocked to avoid boundary-hugging loopholes. Shared edges between Defender regions must not create a seam that allows crossing the line. The mockup and geometry tests must make this understandable; do not require the user to choose an intersection algorithm.
- Recommended old-save policy: existing saved Defenders gain the new behavior on load without renaming roles; grandfather valid airborne batches and cancel newly blocked queues while retaining garrison troops. If preserving exact old-round movement rules is preferred, snapshot/version the blocking policy instead. The user has not chosen between these approaches.
- Multiple Defenders sacrifice multiple Production cities, so preserving the existing shield numbers and AI producer safeguards is a starting balance hypothesis, not proof that border defense is balanced.

## Implementation Plan

### 1. Confirm Policies and Approve Border Mockup
Description: Resolve the open questions and record the chosen boundary/queue/save policy. Create an isolated mockup using the existing Defender control: one Defender protecting a Courtyard approach, several forming an open-field border, gaps, direct attacks, blocked destinations, captures and aircraft bypass. Show shields/relay/network markings together and the actual compact control strip at phone/desktop sizes. Obtain explicit visual approval before production UI changes; do not implement gameplay in this slice.

### 2. Logical Regions and Transit Queries
Description: After slice1, provide deterministic logical-region and route-intersection queries shared by simulation and rendering rather than importing screen-dependent state into core physics. Identify the first blocker for a faction/route, implement target-Defender exemptions and the agreed boundary policy, and compose with existing river/mountain legality. Unit-test connected borders, open ends, multiple blockers, vertex/edge cases, neutral policy and invariance across viewport/DPR. Leave existing movement behavior unchanged until command integration.

### 3. Commands and Launch Queue Enforcement
Description: After slice2, apply shared legality to public ground commands and the chosen unlaunched-batch policy at conversion/capture and launch boundaries. Preserve already-launched troops and unchanged Defender shields/costs/conversion rules. Test invalid-order queue preservation, role removal reopening access, faction ownership switching, partial launches, exact event timing and conservation. Keep attacks on reachable Defenders possible, never redirecting attacks automatically.

### 4. Forecast, AI and Worker Integration
Description: After slice3, replay dynamic border changes and queue cancellation through real forecasts. Update ground/staging/threat queries and aircraft opportunity checks; revalidate stale worker actions through public commands. Add feasible breach/around/air strategies, useful multi-Defender construction and producer/home/relay safeguards without relaxing unrelated role limits. Verify deterministic live/forecast/synchronous/worker results and no resource advantages.

### 5. Save, Restore and Replay Compatibility
Description: After the agreed policy and slice3, persist any required round policy and preserve exact opening/live Defender and queue state. Implement old-save handling without mistaking grandfathered flights for corrupt orders. Verify paused/hidden/terminal saves, existing Defenders, queues blocked after dispatch, no offline progress, exact restart and relay/aircraft/shield coexistence. Reject truly malformed data without silently losing resources or switching rules.

### 6. Approved Border and Order Feedback
Description: After mockup approval and slices2–5, integrate the protected-region visual, blocker identity and valid-target/blocked-route feedback into Canvas and every input mode. Retain current role controls unless a name change is explicitly approved. Test readable combinations with shields/network/relay, keyboard/screen-reader feedback,44px controls and unchanged Canvas/strip geometry; no new path animation or automatic detours.

### 7. Integrated Verification and Tactical Playtests
Description: After slice6, run the full unit, serial Chromium browser, typecheck/build and production PWA suites. Exercise live multi-Defender borders, conversion/capture breaches, queued-versus-airborne behavior, independent factions and saved-round upgrades. Human-playtest a defended Courtyard southern economy and an open-field border against ordinary and upgrade-advantaged opponents, including expansion/rush/air counterplay. Record balance, density/readability and actual-iPhone limitations separately; adjust tuning only with evidence and approval.

## Open Questions

1. Should the visible role remain named Defender, or be renamed Gate House while keeping its existing role identity?
2. Should neutral Defenders be inactive for transit blocking, or block every faction until captured?
3. When a border closes, should newly blocked unlaunched batches be cancelled, keeping their troops at the source, while launched troops finish normally?
4. Should existing saved rounds gain Defender blocking on load, or retain their old movement rules until a new round is started?
