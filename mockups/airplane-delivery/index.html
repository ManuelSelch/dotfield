How should an airplane deliver troops?

Three top-down treatments. Same trip, same cargo, same combat rules.

Ready
A

Park & unload

The plane stops beside the city. Five-dot groups cross a short gap into it, then the plane returns.

Most familiar · looks like a short ground transfer
B

Circle & release

The plane circles the city, releasing five-dot groups inward as it moves. Once empty, it heads home.

Clearly airborne · more motion around the target
C

Dock & pulse

The plane sits at the city edge. Each pulse transfers five troops directly; no separate troop travel.

Cleanest board · batches shown through feedback
Illustrative only. 100 troops · 20 batches of 5 · 4-second unloading · empty return. Shielded batches are lost. No flight combat or conversion UI in this study.
